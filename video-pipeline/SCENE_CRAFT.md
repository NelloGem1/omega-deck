# SCENE CRAFT — Complete Data Dossier
### How a scene is built, frame by frame. Every element documented.
*OMEGA Cinematics Division — collected, documented, recreated.*

---

## 1. THE SHOT (unit of cinema)

A scene = ordered shots. Each shot has 5 controllable axes:

| Axis | Options | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| SIZE | ECU/CU/MS/WS/EWS | intimacy ↔ context |
| ANGLE | eye-level/low/high/dutch | power dynamics |
| LENS | wide/normal/tele | spatial distortion |
| MOVE | static/pan/tilt/dolly/tracking/crane | energy & time |
| DURATION | 1s–8s typical | pacing rhythm |

**Rule:** average shot length in action ≈ 2–4s. Slow shots = tension; fast cuts = chaos.

## 2. COMPOSITION (what's in frame)

- **Rule of thirds** — subject on intersecting grid lines, never dead center (except deliberate hero shots)
- **Headroom** — space above head ≈ 10% of frame height
- **Leading lines** — roads, corridors, gazes pull the eye
- **Depth layers** — foreground / midground / background (never flat)
- **Negative space** — emptiness creates isolation or dread

## 3. LIGHTING (motivated sources)

| Setup | Look | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| 3-point (key+fill+rim) | modeled, readable | dialogue, clarity |
| Low-key (hard key, deep shadow) | noir, danger | threat, night |
| High-key (even fill) | clean, optimistic | daylight, safety |
| Silhouette (backlit) | anonymous, iconic | entrances, finales |
| Practicals (in-frame lamps) | motivated, warm | interiors |

**Color temperature:** warm (orange) = safe/home · cool (blue) = danger/tech · desaturated = dread.

## 4. COLOR GRADE (the emotional filter)

- **Teal-orange** — blockbuster default (skin warm, shadows teal)
- **Bleach bypass** — desaturated, harsh, war
- **Crushed blacks** — lift shadows = vintage; crush = modern dread
- **Selective color** — one hue pops (the red thread trick)

## 5. CAMERA MOVEMENT VOCABULARY

```
DOLLY IN   — push toward subject = realization, dread builds
DOLLY OUT  — reveal context = loneliness, scale
PAN        — survey the world = establishing
TRACKING   — follow subject = immersion, speed
CRANE UP   — rise = transcendence, victory reveal
HANDHELD   — shake = chaos, documentary realism
WHIP PAN   — violent turn = impact, transition
```

## 6. CONTINUITY RULES (what editors police)

- **180° axis** — camera stays on one side of the action line
- **Eyeline match** — character A looks screen-left → B looks screen-right
- **Match cut** — shape/motion continues across the cut
- **Screen direction** — consistent left-to-right travel

## 7. SOUND DESIGN (half the scene)

- **Room tone** — the silence underneath everything
- **Foley** — footsteps, cloth, weapons (the tactile layer)
- **Score** — leitmotifs per character; silence before impact
- **J-cut/L-cut** — audio leads or trails the picture

## 8. THE MACRO STRUCTURE

```
ESTABLISHING SHOT  → where are we? (wide, static, 3-5s)
MEDIUM SHOT        → who is here? (character enters frame)
CLOSE-UP           → what do they feel? (eyes, hands)
INSERT             → what matters? (the object, the detail)
REVERSING ANGLE    → the threat revealed
CLIMAX             → the hit (fastest cuts, biggest shake)
BUTTON             → aftermath, held frame, fade
```

## 9. RECREATION TARGET (what we render)

- Letterbox 2.39:1 · rule-of-thirds hero · slow dolly-in
- Low-key motivated lighting · teal shadows / warm rim
- Establishing → medium → close-up → threat reveal → impact → button
